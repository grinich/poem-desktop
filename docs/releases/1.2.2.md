More ways to make your daily poem comfortable to read.

- **Five serif typefaces:** Georgia, Palatino, Baskerville, Times New Roman, and Iowan Old Style. The menu offers fonts installed on your Mac.
- **Smaller text:** Smallest and Smaller join the existing text-size choices.
- **Live preview:** See your selected typeface at the chosen text size directly in settings.
- Your preferences apply immediately and stay saved between launches. The larger reader uses your selected typeface, too.

Original line and stanza breaks remain intact. All 6,900 layouts across the saved year of 230 poems passed checks for complete visibility and preserved formatting.

Download **PoemDesktop.dmg**, open it, and drag **Poem Desktop.app** to **Applications**. Open the app again to see its controls. Existing installations update automatically or through **Check for updates**.

For macOS 13 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with Developer ID, notarized by Apple, and stapled for offline verification. **PoemDesktop.app.zip** and **SHA256SUMS** are also included.
